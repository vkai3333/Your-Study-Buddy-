Long-term memory

Anki works best with daily effort

Anki-style spaced repetition is one of the strongest ways to retain information over months or years. It works by bringing cards back near the time you might forget them.

Consistency matters.Complete your due reviews every day. Skipping several days creates a backlog and weakens the benefit of the schedule.
⇩

More flashcards

Browse Anki shared decks

Find decks created by other learners, download an APKG file, and import it from the Create tab. Review imported cards and correct anything that does not match your course.

Open Anki shared decks
✦

Understand, then remember

Combine methods

Use Feynman prompts to explain broad ideas, questions to practice applying them, and Anki to keep important details available in long-term memory.

  • Feynman: explain the whole idea in your own words.
  • Questions: practice recognition and application.
  • Anki: maintain details through spaced review.
