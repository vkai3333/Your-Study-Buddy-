# Study content rules

- Keep unit, chapter, and topic names in navigation and deck-selection interfaces only.
- Never put organizational labels such as "Unit 3," "Empires Expand," or "in the context of [topic]" in an Anki card question or answer.
- Never distinguish related cards with labels such as "part 1," "part 2," or "part 3." Give each prompt enough subject-specific context to stand on its own.
- Write self-contained prompts that ask directly about the historical, scientific, or conceptual subject.
- Apply this rule to every current and future built-in deck and to all deck-generation scripts.
